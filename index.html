Method. Each seat's composite is a weighted average of available signals (missing ones dropped, weights renormalized). Seat totals come from a Gaussian-copula simulation (House correlation 0.31, Senate 0.38) calibrated so FPO-only seat spread matches FPO's published range. Fundamentals = 2024 presidential margin (0.5) + 2024 House result (0.3) + 2022 House result (0.2) + national shift; district SD 5.5, Senate and governor SD 6.5. TX, FL, OH, NC, MO, CA, UT, LA and TN were redrawn, so both House-result terms are dropped; AL, NY and GA drop 2022 only; districts without a results table use the presidential margin only. GA, NH, MN and NC are treated as Democratic holds and FL and LA as Republican holds, not modeled. The table lists the 66 closest House districts; the other 369 are treated as safe (207 Democratic). Fundamentals reflect district lean only, not candidate quality, fundraising or scandals.
